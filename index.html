1. Quick Start & Operating Overview

Getting your MML VU-Deck powered, connected, and calibrated

⚡
Power Requirements
  • Power Input: 5V DC via standard USB-C port on the device.
  • Current Draw: ~80mA to 130mA peak (both displays active). Any standard USB port, PC port, or 5V power bank is suitable.
  • Clean Power Note: If needles bounce when silent, adjust the Noise Cutoff slider in settings.
🎙️
Audio Input Routing
  • Mono Mode (Default): Primary audio sensor is duplicated across both displays for mirrored left/right visual metering.
  • True Stereo Mode: Primary input drives the Left Channel, secondary input drives the Right Channel.
  • Acoustic Sensing: Compatible with line-in audio and high-sensitivity acoustic microphone modules.
🖥️
Dual OLED Display System
  • High-Speed Visual Engine: Dual monochromatic OLED screens running at up to 80 FPS.
  • Left Display: Primary channel audio meter & on-device menu navigator.
  • Right Display: Secondary channel audio meter & live settings value inspector.

5. Audio Calibration & Physics Tuning

Optimizing sensor responsiveness and needle dynamics for your acoustic environment

🎛️
Gain Calibration Guide
  • Normal Speech / Moderate Music: Recommended gain setting is 6x to 12x.
  • Quiet Rooms / Distant Audio: Increase gain to 16x to 28x.
  • Loud Speakers / Near Subwoofers: Decrease gain to 2x to 4x to avoid clipping at 100%.
🔇
Noise Floor Cutoff
  • Purpose: Subtracts baseline background noise (e.g. PC fans, AC power ripple).
  • Setting: Keep between 1% and 4% for maximum sensitivity without ghost deflection when silent.
📐
Physics Dynamics Models
  • EMA Dual-Rate: Ultra-fast attack (15ms) with natural logarithmic gravity decay.
  • PI Lively: Proportional-Integral model simulating mechanical needle overshoot (like real analog coil meters).
  • PI Smooth: Overdamped response with heavy fluid damping for smooth, chill movements.

6. Diagnostics & Troubleshooting FAQ

Solutions to common wiring, I2C, and calibration issues

❓
Only One Display Turns On
Ensure both SH1106 displays are powered and wired to SDA 20 / SCL 21. Check the back of Display 2: the I2C address selection resistor must be resoldered from 0x78 (0x3C) to 0x7A (0x3D).
❓
Needles Jitter When Room is Silent
Your USB power supply may have ground ripple. Increase the Noise Cutoff to 3% - 5% in settings, or power the ESP32-C3 from a clean battery / PC USB port.
❓
Touch Button Is Too Sensitive
The capacitive touch sensor works best when calibrated behind an insulated enclosure (plastic, acrylic, or wood chassis).
